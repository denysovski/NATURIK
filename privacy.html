Privacy policy

We collect the minimum needed to deliver your order and answer your messages. We never sell your data.

What we collect and why

  • Orders — name, delivery address, email and phone, to ship the parcel and send updates (contract).
  • Messages — what you write through the contact form, to reply (legitimate interest).
  • Newsletter — your email, only with your consent; unsubscribe in one click.

How long we keep it

Order records for 10 years (Czech accounting law), messages for 2 years, newsletter data until you unsubscribe.

Who processes it

The payment provider (card data never reaches us), the courier (name and address) and our email provider. All within the EU or under EU standard contractual clauses.

Your rights (GDPR)

Access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction and objection — write to privacy@naturik.studio. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority (ÚOOÚ).

Demo store notice: on this prototype nothing you type is sent anywhere — the bag, the checkout and the newsletter all stay in your browser.
